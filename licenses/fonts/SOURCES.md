# Bundled fonts

Original variable WOFF2 files from Google Fonts, downloaded unchanged on 2026-10-17.
Families: Baloo 2 (600–800), Plus Jakarta Sans (400–800).
Subsets: Latin, extended Latin and Vietnamese. No font CDN is needed at runtime.

CSS source: https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Baloo+2:wght@600..800&family=Plus+Jakarta+Sans:wght@400..800&display=swap

SIL Open Font License 1.1 texts are shipped beside this file:
- Baloo 2: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/baloo2
- Plus Jakarta Sans: https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/main/ofl/plusjakartasans

The WOFF2 files are in src/assets/fonts; Vite fingerprints and bundles them under the deployment base path.
