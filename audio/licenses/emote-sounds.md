# Toy throws and expressive reactions

Downloaded 2026-10-17. All sources below are CC0 1.0:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
Original Ogg files are copied unchanged. Playback mixes short excerpts with
cartoon pitch/speed and rhythm changes; no TTS or generated speech is used.

## Wet sounds — rubberduck
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/40-cc0-water-splash-slime-sfx

- slime_12.ogg -> emote-egg-wet.ogg (egg liquid splat)
- splash_01.ogg -> emote-splash.ogg (bucket splash)
- bubble_02.ogg -> emote-kiss.ogg (short bubbly kiss accent)

## Tiny creatures sounds — fvcalderan
Source: https://opengameart.org/content/tiny-creatures-sounds
- battlecry1.ogg -> emote-angry.ogg
Existing laugh.ogg, rival-cry.ogg and eject-cry.ogg from the same author are
arranged into laugh, sob, gasp, smug chuckle and angry/raging shouts. The author
recorded their own voice and applied effects. These are wordless cartoon
performances; Vietnamese labels are expression names, not a voice transcript.
