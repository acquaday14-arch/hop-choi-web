## Runtime mix

Horse movement alternates physical chip and wooden contacts. Dice use grab,
shake and table impact recordings around their natural pitch. UNO rotates
paper slides and placements, with fan/shove accents for reverse, skip and
penalty actions. Ordinary actions no longer trigger automatic mockery voices
or power-up sounds. Contact timing, reduced motion, mute, tab hiding and
audio cancellation follow the existing game lifecycle. No audio variation
consumes gameplay randomness.
