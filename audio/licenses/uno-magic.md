# Galaxy UNO opening audio

The voice files below are human dictionary recordings, copied byte-for-byte.
No TTS is used. The recorded words are "Welcome" and "Enjoy"; the Galaxy UNO
name and Vietnamese greeting are visual text, not claimed voice transcripts.
Playback adjusts volume and speed/pitch to 1.12x (Welcome) / 1.2x (Enjoy)
and layers separate effects.
